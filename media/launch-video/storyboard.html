SPINE · PROPS · RULES

Spine: the ink line, left to right; the camera never cuts until the end card. Hero props: the clock (top left) and the status (top right), on screen from frame 2 to 6. Direction: everything travels right; only the tracking ticket flies left, back to Maya. Motion: travel, stamp, unfold, count-up, stroke-draw; ease-out settles, no bounce. Bans: cards in a grid, UI screenshots, glow, gradient text. Truthfulness: Maya and Ada are illustrative; the envelope is designed, not a screenshot of any email client; nothing is a capture of the live app.
