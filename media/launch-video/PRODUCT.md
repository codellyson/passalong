# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Leads and founders of small teams (up to about ten people) that mix people and AI agents: support, marketing, product and developers working together. Their situation: everything arrives at once (customer emails, bug reports, alerts, requests) and things get dropped. Their job: make sure each one is handled by the right person or agent and closed. This launch film speaks to them first; developers with agents and support people are secondary.

## Product Purpose

Passalong keeps a team's work and what it knows, for people and their agents. Everything that comes in becomes one piece of work. A person or an agent takes it and follows it to Done with proof, and what was learned goes into the project's documents. Success: nothing that came in is left behind, and it is done fast.

## Positioning

One thing called work, worked the same way by people and agents: taken, progressed, handed in with proof a person can check. Incoming things are sorted by Passalong's own agent to the right project and person or group, with its reason. Messages to a customer are drafted by an agent and sent only when a person presses Send.

## Operating Context

Work arrives by the hub's box, email, a phone's share sheet, webhooks and customers writing to a support address (who get a tracking number). Teams see one board (Draft, To do, In progress, Done) and a "Needs you" first screen. Agents connect through the CLI and MCP tools; Passalong never hosts agents, teams bring their own.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Words people see: work, project, document, file, proof. Never "guide". Statuses: Draft, Ready (To do), In progress, Done. Finishing needs proof (a screenshot, or a command and its output). Customer mail goes out only after a person presses Send.

## Brand Commitments

Name: Passalong, at passalong.dev. Hero line: "Get things done fast. Leave nothing behind." Landing world: white ground, ink #252522, coral #f2805d (text coral #b8481f), Onest and Newsreader. Plain, business-friendly words; no developer jargon in marketing.

## Evidence on Hand

No real customers, testimonials or benchmarks may be cited. Story examples (Kemi, Leo, the double charge) are illustrative and must read as such. No screenshots of real customer email.

## Product Principles

- Nothing left behind: every incoming thing ends Done or is visibly still open.
- Proof over claims: finished means shown, not said.
- People decide what reaches customers; agents do the work.
- One vocabulary everywhere: work, project, document, file.

## Launch film notes

The 1.0 launch film must not feel like a generic SaaS explainer (floating UI cards, gradients, stock motion).
